The problem

A spreadsheet never tells you something is wrong

It sits there looking finished. It has no idea that three tickets lapsed last month, that the operator you moved to nights was never signed off on the line, or that your auditor arrives in nine days.

Certificates expire in silence

Forklift, first aid, food hygiene, allergen awareness — all with a date nobody is watching. You find out during the audit, or after the incident.

A daily reminder engine warns managers at 90, 60 and 30 days, then emails on expiry.

Evidence lives in six places

Certificates in a filing cabinet, sign-off sheets in a drawer, a training log on a shared drive, and the matrix itself in someone's Downloads folder.

Evidence attaches to the record it proves, and links to the clause that demands it.

It's stale the day you build it

People join, move team, get signed off, leave. Maintaining a grid by hand is a job nobody owns, so it quietly stops being true.

Assessments are recorded once, in seconds, and the whole matrix recolours itself.

Audit prep becomes a project

Two weeks of chasing paper to reassemble what you already knew, because nothing was organised the way the standard is written.

Evidence is mapped clause by clause, so the pack assembles itself as you work.

How it works

Live matrix in an afternoon, not a quarter

There is no implementation project and no consultant. Three steps, and the first one starts from a template rather than a blank page.

Build the matrix

Import your people from CSV, then pick the industry template closest to your operation and trim it. You are editing a starting point, not inventing a taxonomy.

  • Six industry templates, pre-populated with categories and skills
  • Bulk CSV import for people
  • Binary skills (competent / not competent) and 0–4 levelled skills
  • Group people into teams and set the skills each team requires

Assess and let it watch

Record an assessment with a level, an assessor, a date and — where it matters — an expiry. From then on the system does the watching, not you.

  • Assess one person or a whole team in a single pass
  • RAG status recalculates against expiry and the team's required level
  • In-app and email reminders at each warning threshold
  • Monday digest of everything expired or expiring within 90 days

Prove it on demand

When the auditor asks, the answer is already assembled — by person, by team, by skill, and by the clause of the standard that required it.

  • Gap analysis: who is missing what, ranked by risk
  • Full assessment history with assessor and date on every record
  • CSV export and clean print output
  • Clause-by-clause evidence with the Compliance module

The Skills Matrix module

Everything the grid should have been doing all along

Included on every plan, free one included. The differences between plans are how many people and skills you track — not whether the product works.

The live matrix

People down the side, skills across the top, every cell coloured by real status. Filter by team, category or skill and see the whole operation in one screen.

RAG status with rules, not opinions

Green when current and at level. Amber inside the warning window. Red when expired or below the minimum the team requires. Grey when never assessed. The same rules everywhere, every time.

Expiry reminders that actually send

A scheduled engine runs daily and fires once as each assessment crosses 90, 60 and 30 days — configurable per skill — to team managers, admins, and optionally the person themselves.

Gap analysis

Set the skills each team requires and the minimum level for each. Gap analysis then lists exactly who is short, where, and by how much — the single-points-of-failure view auditors ask for.

Assessment history that holds up

Assessments are append-only. Every record keeps the level, the assessor, the date, and any notes, so you can show progression over time instead of a single overwritten cell.

Skills library & categories

One curated library rather than six spellings of the same certificate. Group into categories, mark which skills expire, and set the warning thresholds that suit each one.

Templates & bulk import

Six industry starting points — manufacturing, food, healthcare, construction, retail, office — plus CSV import for people and bulk assessment for a whole team in one pass.

Export and print

CSV export from Starter upwards, plus print-ready views for the pack you hand across the table. Your data never becomes the reason you can't leave.

Roles, invitations & activity log

Admin, manager and viewer roles, email invitations, an in-app notification centre and an activity log of the changes that matter — so competence data isn't wide open to everyone.

The bit that pays for itself

The reminder fires before it matters, and only once

The single most common failure in competency management is that nobody is told. Skills Matrix App treats reminders as an engine, not a toggle.

  • Daily, not on login A scheduled job checks every live assessment with an expiry date, whether or not anyone opens the app that week.
  • Thresholds you choose 90, 60 and 30 days by default, set per skill. A three-year forklift ticket and a one-year first-aid certificate don't need the same lead time.
  • The right people The managers of every team the person belongs to, plus your admins — and the person themselves, in-app if they have a login and by email if they don't.
  • Once per threshold, never a flood Each event is de-duplicated, so a missed run is caught up on the next one instead of skipped — and nobody gets the same warning twice.
  • A Monday digest One summary email per manager and admin listing everything expired or expiring within 90 days. Switch it off per organisation if you'd rather not have it.

FAQ

The questions buyers actually ask

Do I need a credit card to start?

No. The Free plan is free forever for up to 5 employees and needs no card. Paid plans include a 14-day trial that also starts without a card — you only enter payment details if you decide to continue.

Do I pay for every employee on the matrix?

No. Employees are tracked records, not licences. Your plan limits how many people you can track and how many admin and manager logins you have. Shop-floor operators who never sign in cost nothing per head — which is the opposite of how most competency tools price.

Which audit standards does the Compliance module support?

Clause libraries ship today for BRCGS Packaging Materials (Issue 7), ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015 and ISO 45001:2018.

Further BRCGS standards — Food Safety, Storage & Distribution, Agents & Brokers and Consumer Products — are on the roadmap. The clause, evidence and readiness engine itself is standard-agnostic, so adding a library is content work rather than a rebuild.

Will I actually be warned before a certificate expires?

Yes — this is the part most tools get wrong. A scheduled engine runs daily. As each assessment crosses a warning threshold (90, 60 and 30 days by default, configurable per skill) it notifies the person's team managers and your admins in-app, and emails on expiry. A Monday digest summarises everything expired or expiring within 90 days.

Each event fires once per assessment per threshold, so nobody is spammed — and a missed run is caught up on the next one rather than skipped.

Can I move my existing spreadsheet across?

Import your people from CSV, then start from one of six industry skill templates — manufacturing and engineering, food production and FMCG, healthcare and care, construction and trades, retail and hospitality, or office and professional services — and edit from there rather than typing a taxonomy from scratch.

Most sites have a usable matrix the same afternoon.

Can I get my data back out?

Yes. CSV export is available from Starter upwards and is never withheld to force an upgrade. There is a full organisation export and a subject access export for individual records.

If a paid plan lapses, your organisation moves down to the Free plan rather than being locked out, and your data stays intact.

Is it GDPR compliant?

Skills Matrix App is operated by Conryx Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales. A Data Processing Agreement, privacy policy and cookie policy are published, cookie consent is collected before non-essential cookies are set, subject access exports are supported, and access is limited by admin, manager and viewer roles.

Do I have to buy the Compliance module?

No. It is an optional add-on at £49 per month excluding VAT, independent of your Skills Matrix plan, with a 14-day trial.

The skills matrix works perfectly well on its own. When the module is on, the two share people, teams, notifications and the activity log — and the compliance training register fills itself from your assessments.

Does it work on a tablet on the shop floor?

Yes — the app is responsive and runs in the browser on a tablet or phone, with no install and nothing to deploy to devices. It is a connected web application, so it needs a network connection; there is no offline mode today.
